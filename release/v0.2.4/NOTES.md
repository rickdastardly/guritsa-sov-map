**The first official release of the Gurista - Sov Map desktop app for Windows.**

A live EVE Online sovereignty map in its own window. It has everything on [gurista.co.uk/sov-map](https://gurista.co.uk/sov-map/), plus desktop extras.

### Download
| File | What |
|---|---|
| **GuristaSovMap-Setup-0.2.4.exe** | Installer. Start menu and desktop shortcut, uninstaller, no admin rights needed. **Most people want this.** |
| **Gurista-Sov-Map-0.2.4-windows.zip** | Portable: unzip anywhere and run `GuristaSovMap.exe`. Includes README, changelog and licence. |
| GuristaSovMap-0.2.4.exe | The portable app on its own. |
| SHA256SUMS.txt | Hashes to check your download (`Get-FileHash` in PowerShell). |

Windows 10/11 64-bit. The only other thing it needs is Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is already part of Windows 10/11. The first time you run it, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's in it
- **Sovereignty:** territory by alliance or coalition, ADM, security, sov fights, changes, history playback, true in-game star positions.
- **Live kills and combat:** kill flashes, battles, gatecamps, PvP heat over 1h to 90 days.
- **Activity:** jumps, ship kills, pod kills and NPC kills per system.
- **Route and jump planners:** safer or shortest routes, avoid camps, Thera and Turnur, capital jump ranges and fuel.
- **Overlays:** incursions, faction warfare, wormhole connections, industry index.
- **Killboard and Sov Index**, plus **Intel** (paste Local or d-scan).
- **New: intel channels.** Reads your EVE chat logs and pins every report on the map with its age and how many jumps it is from you. When hostiles are reported close, it plays a sound and flashes the taskbar.

### Desktop extras
- Remembers its size and position.
- F11 for fullscreen.
- Links open in your normal browser.
- Works offline from cache.
- **Updates itself:** it checks a tiny file every 6 hours and only downloads when there's a new version. Map features land instantly from the website, with no app update needed.

No account, no tracking. Chat logs are read on your PC only.
